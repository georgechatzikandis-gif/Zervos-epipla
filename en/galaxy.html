Galaxy coffee table - Ceramic

The Galaxy table set combines two materials and textures, creating a striking look. It consists of two round tables with a ceramic top, which adds a modern character and durability.

The two tables can be placed together or apart, offering flexibility in decorating and everyday functionality. The harmonious contrast of materials and their elegant round shape make the Galaxy set the ideal choice for living rooms with a contemporary, minimal style.
