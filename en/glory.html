Glory wall unit

The Glory wall unit conveys harmony and symmetry through its clean, geometric design, expressing a contemporary style with subtle energy. Its practical storage easily covers everyday needs, while its structure blends naturally into the living room without overwhelming it.

Its versatile layout lets it serve both as a complete wall unit and as a TV unit, offering practicality and flexibility. The closed and open elements keep the room organised while showing off books and decorative items.

Contemporary yet timeless, Glory is the ideal choice for living rooms looking for a simple design, a solid presence and a balance between functionality and character.

L × W × H 300 cm × 45 cm × 210 cm
