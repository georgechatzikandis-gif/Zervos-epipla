Mirror Q60

The Q60 mirror has an organic, asymmetric shape and a wide two-tone frame, with a light inner band and a dark outline. The contrast between the two shades gives it a bold, modern character and makes it stand out like a work of art on the wall.

Hidden lighting around the mirror creates a warm, atmospheric light. Ideal for a hallway, living room or bedroom, it suits contemporary and industrial spaces.
