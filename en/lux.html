Lux coffee table

The Lux coffee table is a refined contemporary design for those who seek luxury in the details.

The solid oak base has been specially bent, giving the piece a unique flow of lines and highlighting its natural character.

The ceramic top, with a texture reminiscent of white marble with gold veins, adds a sense of refined elegance while remaining resistant to heat, scratches and stains.

The rounded corners and vertical texture on the base create an organic minimalist look, ideal for any space seeking modern luxury without excess.

Lux is not just a table — it's a statement piece that turns your living room into a space of beauty and balance.

Dimensions
L × W × H 120 cm × 80 cm × 37 cm
