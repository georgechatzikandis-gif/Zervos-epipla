Magic dining table

The Magic dining table stands out for its contemporary design and the architectural elegance of its base, combining aesthetic excellence with practical functionality. Its top, made of high-quality wood, highlights the natural texture and lasting durability of the material through a careful, refined finish that conveys warmth and a premium character.

The striking lacquered wooden base features curved lines and dynamic geometry, creating a sculptural presence that transforms the room. The harmonious mix of materials and forms offers stability, balance and a bold design-statement character.

Product features
Oak dining table with grey textured-lacquer details. It takes up to 3 extension leaves, each 45 cm long.

L × W × H 137 cm × 107 cm × 75 cm
