Cloud coffee table set

The Cloud table set captures the idea of natural flow within a space.

With organic, wavy lines and a combination of two tables of different heights, it offers flexibility and dynamic balance to the living room layout.

The solid oak base, with its distinctive vertical ribbed texture, pairs beautifully with the marble-look ceramic top, creating a look that exudes calm, luxury and naturalness.

Cloud can work as a single sculptural piece or in separate arrangements, depending on the mood and the space — a set that adapts elegantly to the needs of contemporary design.

Dimensions
Small table 85 cm × 80 cm × height 37 cm
Large table 130 cm × 95 cm × height 41,5 cm
