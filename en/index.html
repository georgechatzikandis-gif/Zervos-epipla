Furniture store in Rhodes

Zervos Furniture (ΖΕΡΒΟΣ Έπιπλα) is a family furniture store on Leoforos Eleftherias in Kremasti, Rhodes, near Rhodes airport, serving the whole island since 1966: Rhodes Town, Ialysos, Ixia, Paradisi, Afantou, Faliraki, Lindos and the Dodecanese.

Visit our showroom for sofas and corner sofas, coffee tables, bedroom furniture and upholstered beds, dining tables, sideboards and chairs, wall units and TV units, kids' and teen bedrooms and LED mirrors. Many pieces can be made to order in the size, fabric and colour you choose. We speak English, so feel free to call, email or visit us.
