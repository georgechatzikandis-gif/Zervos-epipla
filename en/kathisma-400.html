Chair 400-01

The definition of the Comfort Line. Chair 400-01 is the starting point of a collection based on clean lines and complete comfort. The combination of the projection and curve of the back creates a hug-like feeling when seated, offering ergonomic support and a restful experience even after hours of use.

Its understated look makes it highly versatile, blending harmoniously into both contemporary and more timeless spaces. The sturdy solid-wood construction, combined with careful upholstery, shows the attention given to detail and build quality.

Designed for demanding professional and home spaces, 400-01 balances functionality and style, a timeless choice that keeps both its looks and its comfort over time.

Dimensions
Width 51 cm
Depth 60,5 cm
Height 90 cm
Seat height 48 cm
